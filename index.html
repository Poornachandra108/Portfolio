01 — Profile

The analyst behind the models

Open to Marketing Science roles

  • Experience4+ years across gaming, wellness, D2C & entertainment

I’m a Marketing Data Scientist who spends most of the week on one question: did that spend actually cause anything? Platform dashboards are happy to take credit for conversions that would have happened anyway — my job is to separate the two.

At Dave & Buster’s I own the marketing measurement stack: a geo-granular Marketing Mix Model over a $5M monthly media portfolio, geo-holdout incrementality tests across 50 matched markets, and propensity models scoring 20M+ loyalty members. Before that I built MMM and multi-touch attribution at HealthifyMe and revenue & retention models on a $30M gaming portfolio at Gameskraft.

The part I care about most is the last mile: taking a model output and turning it into one sentence a CMO can make a budget decision on.

Measure causally

Geo-holdouts, matched markets and randomised experiments — designed with power analysis first, not rationalised afterwards.

Engineer the pipes

Paid, web, and CRM data consolidated in Databricks so the model, the dashboard and the deck all read from one source.

Validate before you trust

Every model output gets checked against an experiment. When the two disagree, the experiment wins.

Translate to decisions

Weekly reviews with marketing leadership where the deliverable is a reallocation recommendation, not a chart.

Away from the models, I’m happiest somewhere with a scoreboard. I’ve represented my state in badminton, never turn down a game of cricket, and I’m the person who has already checked the qualifying times before a Formula 1 race weekend.

I shoot wildlife photography when I can get out with a lens, dance when I need to reset, and consider dogs non-negotiable company. I’m also a committed foodie — new cuisine, new city, I’m in.

Curiosity, laughter and purpose, roughly in that order. It’s the same instinct that makes me good at the day job: I genuinely want to know why the number moved.
