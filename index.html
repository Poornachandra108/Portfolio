I’m a marketing analyst. Most weeks that means living inside campaign performance, channel efficiency and the funnel — working with marketing leadership on what to run, what to cut, and where the next chunk of budget should go.

What I add is the layer underneath. I’m comfortable building the marketing data science that answers questions reporting can’t: media mix models, geo-holdout experiments, attribution and propensity scoring. It means I don’t have to take a platform’s word for what it delivered, and I don’t have to hand the hard questions to someone else.

Four years in, across gaming, health & wellness and entertainment. The part I enjoy most is the last mile — turning an analysis into one sentence a marketing lead can actually decide on.

Own the performance picture

Campaign, creative and channel reporting across paid, web and owned — and the weekly conversation that comes with it.

Test before believing

Geo-holdouts and randomised experiments, so channel credit is something measured rather than self-reported.

Build my own inputs

Comfortable in SQL, Python and the data layer, so the analysis isn’t blocked waiting for a pipeline.

Land the recommendation

The deliverable is a decision — shift spend here, kill that creative, retarget this cohort — not a dashboard.

I shoot wildlife photography when I can get out with a lens, dance when I need to reset, and consider dogs non-negotiable company. I’m also a committed foodie — new cuisine, new city, I’m in.

Curiosity, laughter and purpose, roughly in that order. It’s the same instinct that makes me good at the day job: I genuinely want to know why the number moved.

03 — Case studies

Work I’d want to talk through

Six pieces of work from the last four years, picked because the method was interesting — not because the slide looked good.

Dave & Buster’s

Geo-Holdout Incrementality Testing

Paid social switched off in a set of markets, matched to controls on pre-period trend correlation and adjusted for seasonality and promotional overlap. The point was to find out what the platform was taking credit for versus what it actually caused.

Geo-experimentsMatched marketsCausal inferenceR
Dave & Buster’s

Geo-Granular Media Mix Model

A market-level MMM decomposing sales into base demand and each channel’s contribution, controlling for seasonality and promotions. Outputs were checked against live geo-experiments before anyone was asked to move budget on them.

MMMRegressionBudget allocationPython
Dave & Buster’s

Loyalty Segmentation & Propensity Scoring

RFM segmentation across the loyalty base, plus a response model scoring each cohort on how likely it was to convert on an offer. That score became the rule for who received which incentive, instead of blanketing everybody.

RFM segmentationPropensity modellingCRMSQL
Dave & Buster’s

Owned-Channel Holdout Experiments

A standing framework for email and SMS: hold a randomised slice of the audience out of every send, then read the difference. It turned owned channels from “we sent it and sales happened” into a measured contribution, and gave content and timing tests a clean baseline.

Experiment designLifecycleSFMCPower analysis
HealthifyMe Wellness

Lifecycle Segmentation & Personalisation

CRM records and in-app behaviour stitched into one profile, then clustered into segments that actually behaved differently. Each segment got its own lifecycle journey rather than the same broadcast, and the segments fed straight into the CRM tooling.

ClusteringLifecycle journeysCleverTapPython
HealthifyMe Wellness

Price Elasticity & Listing Experiments

Modelled how demand responded to price across D2C and marketplace listings, then ran A/B tests on the resulting recommendations rather than shipping the model output straight to production. Also covered listing content and placement effects.

Price elasticityA/B testingeCommerceSQL
